# cosmosearch-chunk

Runs cosmosearch extractors safely and turns their output into chunks.

Part of [cosmosearch](https://github.com/cosmobuild/cosmosearch). Runs extractors with limits and panic isolation, and cuts their passages into chunks. Also holds the built-in text and Markdown extractors. Most programs use it through [`cosmosearch`](https://crates.io/crates/cosmosearch).
